About Heartland Soapworks

A small soap studio with big heartland energy.

Heartland Soapworks started with a simple idea: make soap that feels honest and familiar — clean scents, warm comfort, and that “fresh air after the rain” feeling you remember.

What we make

Small-batch bar soap with a focus on a great lather and a gentle, clean feel. Scents are designed to match the Midwest vibe: warm woods, fresh water, bright mornings, and cozy evenings.
